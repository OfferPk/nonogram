How to play

Each number is a run of filled squares in that row or column, in order, with at least one gap between runs. 3 1 means three filled, a gap, then one filled.

Tap to fill a square. Switch to ✕ to mark squares you know are empty. Drag along a row or column to mark a whole line at once.

Clues turn grey when they're done. Every puzzle can be solved with logic alone, no guessing, and reveals a colour picture.

Big grids: pinch to zoom and pan with two fingers, or use the Zoom button.
