"Quillpix" is our own brand (a quill, as in pencil and paper, plus pixel pictures). It is the sister brand of Quillnine (our sudoku). A web search on 28 Sep 2026 found no game or app called Quillpix. The only hits were an unrelated hobby Android note-app repository on GitHub ("QuillPix-Note-App") and a small creative agency ("Quillpix Creative Company"). Neither is a game or on Google Play. "Nonogram" is the generic name of the puzzle and is used only as a descriptor. We rejected "Gridbloom" (a "Gridbloom Nonogram Picross" already exists), "Pixloom" (a photo editor) and "Quiltwise" (a quilting calculator). The title avoids the names of existing nonogram apps and trademarks (for example "Picross" as a brand title, "Nonograms Katana", "Pictopix").

## Full description (limit 4000)

**2615 / 4000 characters** (plain text, no emoji)

```
Quillpix is a calm, clean nonogram (also called picross or griddlers) for quiet minutes. Use the number clues to find which squares to fill, and a hidden pixel picture slowly appears. When the last square is right, the picture blooms into colour.

HOW TO PLAY
• The numbers beside each row and above each column tell you the runs of filled squares in that line, in order.
• Tap to fill a square, switch to the X tool to mark squares you know are empty.
• Drag along a row or column to fill or cross a whole run in one stroke.
• Clue numbers grey out when their line is satisfied, so you always know where you stand.
• Pure logic, no guessing: every puzzle has exactly one solution you can reach line by line.

348 ORIGINAL PUZZLES IN 11 PACKS
• First Steps: tiny 5×5 pictures to learn the ropes.
• Little Things: 8×8 everyday objects.
• Garden, Seaside, Night & Sky, Cozy Home, Animal Friends, Sweet Treats and On the Road: 10×10, 12×12 and 15×15 pictures.
• Quilt Mosaics: calm symmetric patterns from 10×10 to 20×20.
• Grand Canvas: big 20×20 pictures for a longer, deeper solve.
All pictures are original pixel art made for Quillpix. Each puzzle is checked by a line solver before it ships, so it always has one unique, logical solution.

DAILY PUZZLE
• A new picture grid every day, created from the date. It works offline and is the same for everyone.
• Keep your daily streak going.

MADE FOR FOCUS
• Pinch to zoom and pan on big grids. The clues follow along.
• Undo and redo as many moves as you like.
• Choose your style: a gentle 3-mistake mode, or free mode with no checking at all.
• Auto-cross finished lines and highlight the current row and column (both optional).
• Timer with pause (you can hide the timer in Settings).
• Auto-save: leave any time and continue right where you stopped.

STATISTICS
• Puzzles solved, flawless solves, best and average times for every grid size, and daily streaks.

FIVE QUIET THEMES
• Paper and Midnight (dark mode) are free. Sage, Dusk and Blush can be unlocked with coins you earn by solving puzzles.
• Soft sounds and light haptics, both optional.

PLAY OFFLINE
No Wi-Fi needed. Enjoy a picture puzzle on a flight, on the train or before bed.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Quillpix is free and shows ads. Hints (reveal a line) and an extra life come from optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch.
• No in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by solving puzzles and only unlock cosmetic themes.
• No sign-up and no account.
• Privacy policy: https://offerpk.github.io/nonogram/privacy.html

Clear your mind, one calm picture at a time. Download Quillpix and start solving.
```

## Claims check

Every claim matches the current game (v1.0.0):

- **348 original puzzles in 11 packs, 5×5 to 20×20:** `www/js/puzzles.js`, built by `tools/build-puzzles.js` from hand-drawn grids (`tools/art-small.js`) and original vector motifs (`tools/motifs.js`). CI rebuilds it and fails if it differs.
- **One unique solution reachable by line logic:** `test/puzzles.test.js` runs the line solver (`NL.lineSolve`) on all 348 puzzles and checks it completes the exact picture (so the solution is unique), plus a backtracking count for small grids.
- **Daily puzzle from the date, offline, same for everyone:** `NL.dailyPuzzle()` (seed from the local date); tested for determinism and solvability.
- **Fill / X / drag a line, clues grey out, undo/redo, zoom and pan, mistake or free mode, auto-cross, highlight, hideable timer, pause, auto-save, colour reveal:** `www/js/game.js`, `test/input.test.js` and the headless Chrome test.
- **Five themes; Paper and Midnight free; Sage, Dusk, Blush with coins:** `www/js/themes.js` (150 / 300 / 500 coins).
- **Hints and extra life via optional rewarded ads:** only these two buttons call `Ads.showRewarded`.
- **Coins cosmetic only; no in-app purchases:** there is no billing library in the app.
- **No account; works offline:** correct. Ads just don't load without internet.
